The above fella is a digital persona to give myself a face online (without putting my, uh, ACTUAL FACE online, because that's a bad idea). It's something I, myself, drew recently!


Hi!

My name is Colton Sylvia. I want to be a game developer, so I'm doing the New England Institute of Technology's "Early College Program" (or ECP) course, aiming for an associates degree inVideo Game Design and Development, or maybe the bachelor degree if things line up well.

I've been a fan of games for a long time; indie games especially tend to be favorites of mine. Some of my favorites are AntonBlast, Risk of Rain 2 (though, does that count now that it's owned by Gearbox..?), both of the Slime Rancher games, and especially Rain World, Undertale, and Deltarune.

Go buy and play Deltarune. It's really good (it wouldn't have thoroughly lodged itself into my mind if it wasn't).
